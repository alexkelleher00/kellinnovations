How to play

Use arrow keys, WASD, swipe, or the buttons above to slide every tile in one direction. Tiles with the same number merge into one when they collide. Reach 2048 to win — or keep going for a bigger score. Undo rewinds one move at a time (limited per game). The all-time leaderboard only accepts scores from the classic 4×4 board.
